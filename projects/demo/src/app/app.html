ngx-json-render · Angular renderer for json-render

Your model writes JSON. Angular renders it.

Give a model a catalog of your components. It streams back a json-render spec, and ngx-json-render turns it into real Angular components, progressively, with signals. Same spec as the React, Vue, Svelte and Solid renderers.
